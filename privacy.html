Privacy policy

Content Library is a personal tool with a single user, its owner. It uses YouTube API Services.

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's YouTube playlists and video details, and changes the owner's playlists when the owner asks. This data is kept only on the owner's own computer. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising.

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. YouTube's Terms of Service: youtube.com/t/terms.
